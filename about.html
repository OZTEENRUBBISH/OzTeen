How OzTeen got here

OzTeen began with rubbish removal and landscaping jobs, and grew into three businesses run by the same crew, serving the Northern Beaches seven days a week.

Our goal

Growing a team of young locals

OzTeen started with two students and a ute. One day, we want to be busy enough to hire more young, skilled and passionate students.

We want to give other local students what we were lucky to find: real work, real responsibility and a team to learn from, all while they study.
